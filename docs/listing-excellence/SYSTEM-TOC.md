## A. Pre-listing packet leave-behind

Purpose: the seller, co-executor, or attorney can read this before anyone sits down and know who we are, what we do, what we do not do, and how the appointment will work. It is a working paper, not a brochure.

Send as PDF 24 to 48 hours ahead. Print on letter size. Say it may be forwarded.

**Do not put the final recommended list price in this packet.** Put method, and a preliminary set of real homes if Emily has pulled them. The recommendation is said at the house after the walk.

### A2. What is inside each page (so Carrie does not invent blocks)

**1. Cover (CORE).** Brand mark. Prepared for [NAME]. Property address. Meeting day and time. "Take the house off your desk" on PG covers. Hardesty cover line: "A clear plan for selling [ADDRESS]." Team first names and the phone. Footer: licensed real estate, not a law firm, not CPAs. PG adds decedent line only when the family uses that name and Scott has confirmed it is appropriate. Do not print a court case number.

**2. Note (CORE).** Four short paragraphs: thanks, what we do, what we do not do, please mark questions and forward this. Sign-off with who is coming.

**3. Team (CORE).** Three people, one sentence each, one line on who owns the daytime phone. "You are not handed to a call center."

**4. Off the desk (CORE).** Two columns. Left: access, utilities check-in, lawn and basic security coordination, vendor runs, photos, listing updates, showing logistics, offer summaries, paperwork handoff to the attorney or title. Right: decisions that stay with the seller (price path, repairs they choose to pay, what stays in the house, any court or attorney step). PG left column adds clean-out coordination and heir updates. Do not list funded repairs as a feature on this page until terms exist.

**5. House and court (PG).** Plain split. We do not give legal advice, do not file, do not get paid by the law firm. Letters (the court paper that names who can act) come before we list. Sometimes heirs sign too. The attorney tells us which. We can still secure the house and prepare numbers before that paper exists.

**6. Vacant house (PG).** What an empty house needs someone to watch: locks and keys, utilities, lawn, leaks, winter, insurance notice to their own insurer. Carrying cost named as a monthly leak (taxes, insurance, utilities, upkeep) without a made-up dollar figure. "Confirm the insurance with your insurer. We are not insurance agents."

**7. Keepsakes, clean-out, cars (PG).** Order of operations: family chooses keepsakes, then estate sale or clean-out, then the house is photographed. Cars and other personal property can be lined up with the right local help. We coordinate. We do not appraise for the estate inventory. Donation receipts go to the executor for the attorney or CPA.

**8. Same page for everyone (PG).** Who is on the update list. Weekly written summary. Offers summarized the same way to every authorized person. Attorney copied when the executor asks. Portal: login for executor, heir, and attorney once we are engaged. What the portal does in its current version, and what it does not do yet (no promise of automatic text alerts).

**9. Road picture (CORE).** Eight beats matching the roadmap: first call, appointment, decision, prep, live listing, offers, under contract, close and after. PG picture adds a gate: "attorney confirms who can sign" before live listing, and a note that court timing can pause the house without us promising a date.

**10. Two paths (CORE).** Side by side. As-is cash: independent buyers, often faster, usually less money, we are not the buyer, we are not paid more for cash. Open market: list when they can wait, photos and showings, commission at close. Both numbers at the appointment, on one page they can forward. One line: an empty house costs money every month it sits, so speed has a value, and so does the market.

**11. Price method (CORE).** Three groups: recently sold, for sale now, under contract. What we adjust for (condition, size, updates). Slot for 3 to 5 real homes **or** a bridge box: "We will complete this from the walk. Here is what we will show you." No final price. No Zillow screenshot as the valuation.

**12. Marketing this house (CORE).** Checklist with owners: photographer, MLS entry, where it syndicates because of the MLS, showing instructions, feedback, weekly note to the seller, one flyer, agent-to-agent outreach we will actually do. Optional video or drone only if it is booked for this house. Sample strip: real past listing or omit. Vacant-house showing rules on PG (appointment, no open-house circus during a raw grief week unless they ask).

**13. First 30 days (CORE).** Week blocks: agree and paperwork, prep and photo, go live, showings and a written read. Mention Coming Soon/Hold as a possible hold-for-prep status only with a footnote for Carrie: "Scott confirms current Realtracs rules before this box is a promise." See competitive brief.

**14. How we get paid (CORE).** Nothing up front. Listing commission at closing, agreed in writing, not set by law, fully negotiable. Rate left as [AGREED RATE] until Scott fills the live file. Not paid more for cash. Not the buyer. No attorney referral fees. Buyer-agent compensation: buyers have their own agreement; if the seller chooses to pay some or all of the buyer's agent, the amount is written down before it is offered. No surprise management invoice.

**15. What good looks like (CORE).** Expectations list. Proof box marked internal-only until a real quote exists. No star ratings.

**16. Agenda and bring list (CORE).** Agenda: walk, condition, both numbers, marketing, next step. Bring list: who else decides, attorney name, letter status if known, utility accounts if on hand, known repairs, mortgage or HOA if any, questions from people who will not be in the room. PG adds: keys, who has been in the house, keepsake plan, cars on site.

### A3. Explicitly not in the packet

- Awards collage, brokerage corporate brochure, "top 1%" wall
- Final list price or a 20-page valuation novel
- Listing contract (bring it to the appointment if they are ready, do not bind it into the leave-behind)
- Guaranteed offer program, auction brand, or "we will buy it"
- Cookies, gift baskets, logo food
- Stock photos of smiling families
- Other people's testimonials
- Obituary mail or funeral-home language

## B. In-person listing presentation (Gamma)

Purpose: a working appointment, not a pitch deck they watch in silence. **Walk the house first.** Then sit. Target **60 to 75 minutes** including the walk. The deck itself is about **25 to 35 minutes**.

The packet already taught scope. The appointment does five things the packet cannot: see the house, hear what "done" means to them, show real numbers, recommend a path, agree the next step.

### B2. What the presentation must not do

- Open with a bio longer than 60 seconds. The team slide is the bio.
- Hide the price until the end. Say the range after the homes, before marketing.
- Argue them into the higher path. Show both numbers. Ask which problem they are solving.
- "Sign tonight" close. Next step is a clear ask: paperwork if authority is clear and they are ready, or a written recap the same day if they are not.

## C. Full roadmap (operating system)

The seller-facing picture is page 9 of the packet (eight beats). The team runs a finer version. Detail is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). This is the contents page.

| Phase | Name | CORE / PG | Seller sees | Team owns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First contact | CORE, with PG intake questions | A human, a time, no pitch | Shelly or Scott |
| 2 | Before the appointment | CORE + PG packet | PDF, then print | Emily + Carrie template, Scott reviews numbers |
| 3 | Appointment | CORE + PG fluency | Walk, both numbers, next step | Scott, Branton as needed |
| 4 | Listing decision and authority gate | CORE; PG gate is mandatory | Recap the same day. Sign only when the right person can sign | Scott + attorney loop on PG |
| 5 | Prep | CORE; PG adds secure, clean-out, cars | Dated photos, vendor schedule | Branton field, Shelly coord |
| 6 | Listing file and go-live | CORE; PG copies attorney | Listing paperwork in plain language, then MLS | Scott |
| 7 | Marketing | CORE; PG showing rules for vacant | What ran this week | Scott, Shelly on updates |
| 8 | Offers | CORE; PG same grid to all authorized people | One-page comparison per offer | Scott |
| 9 | Under contract | CORE; PG court and remote signing | Deadlines in plain language | Scott, Branton |
| 10 | Close | CORE + PG | What to bring, what they net, keys and utilities | Scott |
| 11 | Aftercare | CORE; PG portal and vendor wind-down | One close note, then a check-in | Shelly |

INTERNAL pre-listing research (adapted from the public Keller Williams "What I Do For You" checklist, not printed): confirm appointment, pull sold / active / pending / expired, tax record and ownership name, lot, HOA if any, exterior look before the meeting, schools only if they affect buyer traffic and only stated carefully. On PG files add: letter status, who may sign, vacancy, keys, utilities, co-executor contacts, attorney name. Do not dump this list into the packet.
