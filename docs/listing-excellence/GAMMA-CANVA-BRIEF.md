# Gamma and Canva brief for Carrie

Build two artifacts from existing words. Do not write new customer copy. If a sentence is missing, it belongs in [PACKET-COPY-DRAFT.md](PACKET-COPY-DRAFT.md) or [PRESENTATION-SCRIPT.md](PRESENTATION-SCRIPT.md), not in a text box you invent on deadline.

| Artifact | Tool | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leave-behind, 12 pages Hardesty or 16 pages Probate Guardians | **Canva** | Letter size, print margins, page-locked PDF, pocket-folder packet. Gamma's slide export is a weak print piece |
| Appointment deck, 16 or 22 slides | **Gamma** | Present mode, big type, speaker notes from the script. You are not printing this as the leave-behind |
| One-page "two paths" and one-page "offer grid" | **Canva masters**, then a matching Gamma slide | These get forwarded by email later, so they must look right on paper, not only on a TV |

Do not maintain two wordings. Paste from the copy draft and the script.

## Brand

**Probate Guardians.** Start from the live site, then ask Scott before you lock a new palette.

- Wordmark: Probate Guardians TN
- Site type: Fraunces for headlines, DM Sans for body. A Canva analogue is fine if those files are not available. Do not use a script font, and do not use a "luxury serif plus gold foil" look
- Tone of the page: quiet, warm paper, lots of margin. This is a kitchen table, not a magazine ad
- Phone on every spread footer: (615) 669-7075

**Hardesty Group.** If Scott has a wordmark, use it on the cover and nowhere else as decoration. If he does not, stop and ask. Do not invent a crest. Interior pages can match the PG system so the two packets are obviously one family, with a different cover line (see packet copy).

**Both**

- Letter size, 0.6 inch margins minimum, so a home printer does not eat the phone number
- Body text at least 12 pt. Executors forward these and print them again
- No em dashes in any text frame. The drafts do not use them. If Canva auto-replaces a hyphen, fix it
- No stock photos of smiling families, handshakes, gavels, or empty suits
- Photography: the house, or nothing. A gray box labeled "Photo after the walk" is better than a staged mansion
- Footer on page 1 and the last page: Scott Hardesty, eXp Realty. Branton Walker, The Forward Realty Group LLC. Licensed real estate. Not a law firm. Not CPAs

**Presenting notes**

- Export PDF backup of the deck in the same folder as the packet, in case Gamma will not open

## Quality bar

Carrie is done with v1 when a stranger can open the PDF and answer, without asking us:

- Who is coming to the house?
- What will you do, and what will you not do?
- When can the house actually be listed, on an estate file?
- What are the two paths, and who gets paid?
- What should I have ready?
- Where is the price? (Answer: at the meeting, on purpose.)

If the PDF cannot answer those, a better photo will not fix it.
