# Listing excellence pack

Research and system design for **Probate Guardians TN** (estate and probate property) and **Hardesty Group** (normal listings). Shared core, probate overlays.

This folder is docs only. It does not change the website, the estate portal, the CRM, or any production feature.

**Audience:** Scott Hardesty (listing path), Branton Walker (partner), Shelly Mulholland (daytime calls and coordination), Emily (coordination), Carrie (Gamma and Canva).

**Date:** September 2026.

## What this pack is for

Carrie should be able to open these files and build a v1 without guessing structure:

- A **print / PDF leave-behind** (pre-listing packet), sent before the appointment and handed over at the house.
- A **screen presentation** Scott or Branton walks in person after they have seen the house.
- A **start-to-finish roadmap** the team actually runs, from first call through aftercare.

The pack does not invent a new business. It organizes listing excellence for the lanes already in use: open-market listing, as-is cash comparison with both numbers, house-side coordination, clean-out after keepsakes, help lining up cars and personal property, family portal when the estate is engaged, and the attorney loop.

Locked lines, both brands:

- Paid a listing commission at close only. Nothing up front.
- Not the buyer. Not paid more if the seller takes a cash offer.

- Small team. Not a call center.
- Two paths with both numbers: as-is cash, and open market.
- Price is said at the appointment from real sales. It is not guessed in the leave-behind.

## Voice

Spoken. Flyer-plain. The way Scott would say it at a kitchen table.

- No em dashes in customer-facing copy. The packet and the script already follow that. Keep it when you paste.
- No listing-form jargon in customer copy. Say "similar homes that sold" before any MLS shorthand. If a form name must appear, it appears once, in parentheses, after the plain words.
- No cheesy sympathy paragraphs. One respectful line is enough. Then be useful.
- No invented testimonials, review counts, sold-estate stories, or CMA numbers. Empty slots are marked **PROOF WE NEED**. Leave them empty.

## What v1 deliberately leaves blank

These are useful later. They are not allowed in v1 until they are real:

- Executor or seller quotes
- "We have sold X probate homes" or any list-to-sale or days-on-market claim
- Before-and-after clean-out photo stories
- A marketing sample page (use a real past listing, or omit the page)
- Named vendors, unless Scott will stand behind that partner this month
- Funded-repair mechanics. The public site mentions funded repairs. This pack does not explain them in customer copy until Scott writes the terms on one page (who pays, when, what happens if the house does not close). Until then, repairs are "a choice we price after the walk," not a product slogan
- Billboard, stadium, radio, or "millions of impressions" claims. Those belong to other brands
- A guaranteed sale price, a guaranteed timeline, or "the court will approve"

Honest line when proof is thin, from the September 2026 Emily brief:

> We're a focused Middle Tennessee team. We'd rather show you how we work on this property than pad a packet with other people's stories.

## Legal boundary for everyone using this pack

Agents are not attorneys and not CPAs. Customer copy may explain, in plain words, that letters and signatures are the attorney's call. It may point people to confirm vacant-house insurance with their insurer. It must not say what the court will do, what a will means, or what something is worth for estate tax or inventory.

Statutes and MLS rules in the competitive brief are for the team's education. They are not paragraphs to paste into the packet.
