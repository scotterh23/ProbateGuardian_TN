Davidson County is Middle Tennessee's urban heart — and when you inherit a home here, the pace can feel relentless. Metro Nashville probate timelines, vacant properties in Madison and Antioch, sibling heirs spread across the country, and a house that still needs insurance, lawn care, and decisions you are not ready to make. Most families have never done this before. You should not have to learn probate real estate while you are still grieving.

Probate Guardians TN serves Davidson County heirs from our Nashville support desk and Branton Walker's field visits. We coordinate with your probate attorney on authority to sell, deliver complimentary CMAs and Net Sheets for Hermitage ranchers and Bellevue cottages alike, and present four paths on your Guardian Kit: as-is cash offers, funded repairs, full MLS listing, or retain-and-rent — always subject to court approval.

Whether the property is a fixer in Antioch or a paid-off home in Old Hickory, we dispatch clean-out crews, secure vacant homes, and run competing buyer bids through our Express Offers network. Out-of-state heirs get photo updates and weekly status — you are never the project manager of your own grief. One call to (615) 669-7075 starts everything.
