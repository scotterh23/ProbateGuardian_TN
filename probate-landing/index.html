What families are going through

Probate property is not a normal real estate transaction — and nobody warns you how heavy it feels.

The house sits empty while life moves on

Vacant homes in Hermitage, Gallatin, or Mt. Juliet need insurance, lawn care, and lock changes — but you're grieving, out of state, or simply overwhelmed.

Siblings see different paths

One heir wants cash now. Another wants to list in Brentwood or Franklin for maximum value. Group texts replace decisions and tension builds.

Court timelines you don't understand

Authority to sell, Muniment of Title, formal administration — the property cannot move forward until someone coordinates with your attorney.

Every drawer is a decision

Attic boxes, garage tools, a childhood bedroom untouched — clean-out, estate sales, and sentimental shipping feel impossible on top of funeral follow-up.
