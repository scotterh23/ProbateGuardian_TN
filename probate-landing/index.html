Muniment of Title Made Simple

A Tennessee shortcut that surprises many families — when the estate qualifies, it can save months.

Muniment of Title (T.C.A. Title 32) is a simplified probate path for estates that have:

  • A valid will
  • No unpaid debts — except those secured by real estate (like a mortgage)
  • Heirs who agree on transferring the property

Instead of a full administration, the court admits the will as proof of title so the property can transfer faster. Your probate attorney determines if you qualify — we never give legal advice.

Our role: coordinate the property side — valuation, buyers, listings, or Muniment-ready timelines — while your attorney handles the court filing.

Every timeline and offer is subject to court approval.
