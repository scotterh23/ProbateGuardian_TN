Senior Move Assistance

Care transitions are hard enough — we lighten the house burden before probate even starts.

Care transitions and estate moves with compassion — before and during probate.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

Sometimes the story begins before probate: Mom needs skilled nursing, Dad’s house is too big to maintain, or hospice has been called and the family realizes no one knows what happens to the property if care lasts months. Senior Move Assistance is our compassionate bridge — movers, packing, downsizing vendors, and introductions to elder-law attorneys for Medicaid planning (always attorney-led, never from us).

Probate Guardians TN helps Middle Tennessee families plan the house burden while caring for people still living. We coordinate with social workers and facility discharge planners, align timelines with eventual estate coordination, and keep the same warm tone Branton brings on field visits across Wilson and Sumner counties.

When the time comes, your Guardian Kit is already familiar — not a stack of surprises. Whether you are in Mt. Juliet, Gallatin, or Nashville proper, one call connects compassion with a plan.

How we help families in Middle Tennessee

  • Wilson County families facing Lebanon skilled-nursing discharge with a house still full of furniture
  • Davidson hospice families — pre-death planning so heirs are not scrambling in crisis mode
  • Sumner heirs relocating a surviving parent to Hendersonville while selling a Portland childhood home
  • Mover and packing referrals with items inventoried for eventual clean-out
  • Medicaid planning introductions — vetted elder-law attorneys only, never our legal advice
  • Branton's field team for gentle in-home walk-throughs at your pace

Local examples

A Wilson County family faced skilled-nursing discharge for Dad while his Lebanon house sat full of furniture. We coordinated movers, introduced an elder-law attorney for Medicaid questions, and lined up eventual estate coordination before crisis mode hit.

In Nashville proper, hospice had been called and adult children realized no one knew what happened to the Hermitage home if care lasted months. We helped plan the house burden early — so probate did not start from zero on the worst day.
