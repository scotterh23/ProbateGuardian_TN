When maximum net is the goal and the family has time for a marketed sale, you deserve a seller's agent who knows probate — not one who treats your inherited home like any other listing. Probate Guardians TN handles MLS launch, probate-specific showing strategy, disclosure coordination with your attorney, and marketing that respects that the seller is grieving.

Commission structure is explained upfront on the Net Sheet — no hidden line items for Williamson or Davidson heirs already wary of being taken advantage of.

This service integrates with our Probate Real Estate Specialist coordination and your county guide for local comp knowledge. Call when you are ready to list with compassion and competence.
