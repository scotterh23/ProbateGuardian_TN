Questions we hear a lot

How do you sell an inherited house in Tennessee?

We walk the house and put three numbers on one page. Cash, the open market, and what you'd net if you fix it first. You pick the path. Your attorney, and the court if needed, still have to sign off.

Do I have to take a cash offer?

No. Cash is one number on the page. If the open market leaves the estate more after the cost of waiting, take it to market.

Are you the buyer?

No. We are licensed agents, not the buyer. Offers come from independent cash buyers we work with.

What if one sibling wants to keep the house?

We still write the same numbers for everyone. We don't take sides. Keeping it is a family decision.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm. Most estates need a Tennessee probate attorney before you can sell. If you do not have one, we can point you to several.

Can I sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. They usually buy it without repairs. If the house is full, we can line up a clean-out so keepsakes come out first. If fixing it first would change the market number, that version is on the same page.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We do the walk-through and the photos. You can review numbers and sign from where you live.
