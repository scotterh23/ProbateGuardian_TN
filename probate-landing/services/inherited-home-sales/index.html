Inherited Home Sales

Three real paths. One Net Sheet. No heir left guessing what they will actually receive.

Sell an inherited house with clarity — as-is cash, funded repairs, or full MLS.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

An inherited home is not just an asset — it is where holidays happened, where someone’s coffee cup still sits in the cabinet, where every sibling has a different memory and a different opinion about what to do next. We honor that emotional weight while giving your family something rare in probate: clarity.

Probate Guardians TN builds a side-by-side Net Sheet for Middle Tennessee properties — list with funded repairs, as-is cash through our Express Offers network, or a sibling buyout scenario — so Murfreesboro, Franklin, and Hendersonville heirs see real dollars, not Zillow guesses. We coordinate with your attorney on court approval timelines and never push a path that does not fit your family’s grief season.

Whether the house is in Lebanon, Mt. Juliet, or a quiet street in Brentwood, you get one Project Coordinator, weekly updates, and vendors dispatched from our rolodex. Explore our county guides for local court flavor — then call when you are ready to compare paths without commitment.
