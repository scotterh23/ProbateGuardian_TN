How we're paid

Nothing is owed up front. If the house sells, we are paid a listing commission at closing. That is true whether you take cash or take it to market. We are not paid more when you take cash.

If the path is a buyout and there is no listing, we will tell you how we are paid before any work starts.

Questions we hear a lot

How do you sell an inherited house in Tennessee?

We walk the house and put the numbers on one page. Cash, the open market, a buyout if someone wants to keep it, and what waiting costs. You pick the path. Your attorney, and the court if needed, still have to sign off.

What if one sibling wants to keep the house?

That is a path, not a problem. We write what a buyout would take so everyone is looking at the same number. We don't take sides.

What if there's still a mortgage or a reverse mortgage?

It changes the net and the timeline. We put that on the page before anyone picks a path. Your attorney and the lender handle the payoff. We don't.

What if a tenant is in the house?

It changes access, timing, and sometimes the number. We note it on the page. We don't give landlord-tenant advice.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm. Most estates need a Tennessee probate attorney before you can sell. If you do not have one, we can point you to several.

What about taxes when we sell?

We are not CPAs and we don't give tax numbers. A sale can have tax consequences. Ask a CPA. We can point you to one.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We do the walk-through and the photos. You can review numbers and sign from where you live. More on the out-of-state executors page.

Can we sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. Cash buyers usually take it as-is. If the house is full, we can line up a clean-out so keepsakes come out first. If repairs would change the market number, that version is on the sheet.
