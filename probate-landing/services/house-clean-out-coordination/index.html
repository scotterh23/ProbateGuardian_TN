House Clean-Out Coordination

The attic, the garage, the closets — emptied with dignity, not dumped in a panic.

Contents, junk, attic, garage — dispatched and tracked for you.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

Walking through a loved one’s home after they are gone can stop you in the doorway. Every drawer is a decision. The garage is twenty years of projects. Siblings want keepsakes shipped to three states. Meanwhile the lawn is growing, the insurance company wants someone on site, and no one has the bandwidth to call ten junk haulers for quotes.

Probate Guardians TN dispatches estate-sale professionals, haul-off crews, and donation partners from our Middle Tennessee rolodex — tracked by your Project Coordinator so Sumner, Wilson, and Davidson heirs are not managing dumpsters from a hotel room in another time zone. Sentimental items are inventoried and shipped; bulk removal is scheduled; $0-upfront options are explored where vendors allow.

Clean-out pairs naturally with cash offers, repairs, or MLS listing — we sequence work so you are not paying for work twice. See your Guardian Kit Step 4 for how contents fit the bigger timeline.

How we help families in Middle Tennessee

  • Nashville-area families needing estate sale + donation receipts for probate accounting

  • Vacant-home prep for Williamson listings — clean, secure, and show-ready without heir labor

Local examples

Wilson County heirs in Lebanon could not face the attic alone — forty years of memories in boxes. We scheduled an estate-sale team and haul-off at their pace, inventoried keepsakes, and shipped photo albums to a sibling in Texas.
