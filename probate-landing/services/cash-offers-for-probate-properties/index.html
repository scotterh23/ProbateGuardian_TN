These are the situations where families usually ask for both numbers. The house might be in Nashville, Mt. Juliet, or Clarksville. The process is the same.

Common questions

How do cash offers work on a probate house in Tennessee?

After we see the house, you get one page with a listing net and cash bids. The cash bids come from independent buyers the brokerage has already vetted. Sales still follow your attorney and, where required, the court.

Are you the buyer?

No. We are licensed agents. The bids come from independent cash investors the brokerage has already vetted.

How fast can a cash sale close?

Bids often come in over a few days. Closing still depends on title, your attorney, and court approval. Cash usually skips a mortgage and listing showings. That is why people compare it when the empty house is costing money.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm. Most estates need a Tennessee probate attorney for authority to sell. If you do not have one, we can point you to several. Attorneys can start on our for attorneys page.

What if cash is lower than listing?

Then listing may be the better path. That is why both numbers are on the same page, along with what it costs to wait. We do not push cash. Franklin, Brentwood, and Murfreesboro houses often do better listed if the estate can wait.

Can I sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. These bids are usually as-is. If the house is full, we can coordinate a clean-out. If repairs plus a listing would net more, that comparison is on the sheet. See house clean-out coordination.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We do the walk-through and the photos. You can review numbers and sign from where you live. More on our out-of-state executors page.
