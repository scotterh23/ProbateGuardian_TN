When cash is the right path

Cash makes sense when the clock is louder than the last few percent of list price. Vacancy insurance, lawn, and utilities keep billing while the house sits — in Gallatin, Hendersonville, Mt. Juliet, Lebanon, Smyrna, Clarksville, Columbia, and everywhere between.

Carrying costs are the clock

A vacant house still needs vacancy insurance, lawn care, and utilities. Those bills do not pause for grief. When months of carrying would eat the listing upside, cash is worth putting on the same sheet.

The house is vacant

Empty homes in Hermitage, Gallatin, or Smyrna invite lawn tickets, pipe risk, and insurance questions. An as-is cash path can stop the vacancy clock without weeks of showings.

Heirs live out of state

You should not have to fly in to walk a Nashville or Clarksville house for every bidder. We handle eyes-on and the photo set here. See help for out-of-state executors.

Siblings do not agree

One heir wants cash now. Another wants to list in Franklin or Murfreesboro. The Net Sheet puts both numbers in writing so the argument is about facts, not a postcard. We do not take sides.

The house needs heavy repairs

Roof, HVAC, tenant damage — traditional buyers often walk. Cash buyers typically take the house as-is. If funded repairs plus a listing would still net more, that path is on the same document. See repair and staging coordination.

How the process works

Six steps. No pressure at any point. Your timeline — and your attorney's — not ours.

  1. 1

    First call — what we need

    The property address and where the estate stands (named executor, attorney already hired, court still ahead). We listen first. Nothing to sign on that call. Call or text (615) 669-7075.

  2. 2

    We get eyes on the house

    An in-person walk and a dated photo set so Express Offers buyers — and you — are looking at the same property. You do not have to be there.

  3. 3

    We run both numbers

    Listing net vs. cash net on one written page, including typical costs and carrying. Share it with heirs and counsel before anyone picks a path. Listing details are on inherited home sales.

  4. 4

    Express Offers — competing written bids

    Independent buyers on eXp Realty's Express Offers list submit written as-is bids. Express Offers typically gathers competing bids within 48–72 hours — days, not weeks. That is typical timing, not a guaranteed SLA. eXp vets who gets on that list. We are not the buyer.

  5. 5

    Attorney and court

    Offers are written subject to court approval where required. We do not practice law. Your probate attorney confirms authority and timing. Counsel is welcome to review every number. Attorneys can start on our for attorneys page.

  6. 6

    Close as-is — clean-out coordinated

    If you choose cash, the sale is typically as-is. We can coordinate clean-out so keepsakes are set aside before haul-away. See house clean-out coordination.
