Questions we hear a lot

How do cash offers work on a probate house in Tennessee?

After we see the house, you get one page with the cash offers and what you'd probably get if you listed. The offers come from buyers the brokerage has already vetted. Your attorney and, if needed, the court still have to sign off.

Are you the buyer?

No. We are licensed agents. The offers come from cash investors the brokerage has already vetted.

How fast can a cash sale close?

Offers often come in over a few days. Closing still depends on title, your attorney, and the court. Cash usually skips a mortgage and showings. That is why people compare it when the empty house is costing money.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm. Most estates need a Tennessee probate attorney before you can sell. If you do not have one, we can point you to several. Attorneys can start on our for attorneys page.

What if cash is lower than listing?

Then listing may be better. That is why both numbers are on the same page, along with what it costs to wait. We do not push cash. Franklin, Brentwood, and Murfreesboro houses often do better listed if you can wait.

Can I sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. They usually buy it without repairs. If the house is full, we can coordinate a clean-out. If repairs plus a listing would leave the estate more, that comparison is on the sheet. See house clean-out coordination.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We do the walk-through and the photos. You can review numbers and sign from where you live. More on our out-of-state executors page.
