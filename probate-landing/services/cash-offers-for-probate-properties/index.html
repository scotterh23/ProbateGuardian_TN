Questions we hear a lot

How do cash offers work on a probate house in Tennessee?

We walk the property, document its condition, and send that package to independent cash buyers we work with. They respond in writing. You see every offer alongside what the estate would likely net on the open market.

Are you the buyer?

No. We are licensed real estate agents. Every cash offer comes from an independent buyer, and we are not paid by them.

How fast can a cash sale close?

Cash removes the financing timeline. It cannot remove the court's schedule where approval is required. We'll give you a realistic window once we know which county the estate is in and what authority you're working under.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm and can't advise you on that. Most executors in this position are working with a Tennessee probate attorney, and if you don't have one we can point you toward several.

What if cash comes in lower than the market number?

That happens often. A lower number today can still leave the estate ahead of a higher number months out, once carrying costs and repairs are counted. The page shows you both.

Can I sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. Cash buyers generally take properties as they stand. If you'd rather clear it out first, we coordinate that too, keepsakes out before anything else moves.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We handle the in-person work and closing documents can be signed remotely.
