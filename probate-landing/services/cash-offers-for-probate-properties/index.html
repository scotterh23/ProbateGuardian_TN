Questions we hear a lot

How do cash offers work on a probate house in Tennessee?

After we see the house, you get one page with both numbers. The offers come from independent cash buyers we work with. Your attorney and, if needed, the court still have to sign off.

Are you the buyer?

No. The offers come from independent cash buyers we work with.

How fast can a cash sale close?

Offers usually come in within a few days. Closing still depends on title, your attorney, and the court. Cash usually skips a mortgage and showings. That is why people compare it when the empty house is costing money.

Do I still need an attorney?

We are not a law firm. Most estates need a Tennessee probate attorney before you can sell. If you do not have one, we can point you to several.

What if cash is lower than listing?

Then the open market may be better. That is why both numbers are on the same page, along with what it costs to wait. We do not push cash.

Can I sell as-is if the house is full or needs work?

Yes. They usually buy it without repairs. If the house is full, we can coordinate a clean-out. If repairs plus the open market would leave the estate more, that comparison is on the sheet.

Do I have to fly in if I live out of state?

No. We do the walk-through and the photos. You can review numbers and sign from where you live.
