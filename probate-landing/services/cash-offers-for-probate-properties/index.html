Cash Offers for Probate Properties

Competing cash bids in days — not one investor's take-it-or-leave-it number.

Multiple competing cash buyers — as-is, no showings, fast certainty.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

Sometimes the house needs to close a chapter fast: out-of-state heirs, costly carrying months, sibling tension, or simply the emotional toll of driving past a childhood home every week. Cash can be the right answer — but one lowball investor email is not a plan. Through eXp Realty’s Express Offers network and our Middle Tennessee investor desk, Probate Guardians TN typically gathers competing as-is bids within 48–72 hours.

You still see everything on a Net Sheet next to funded-repair and MLS paths — because cash is not automatically best for Franklin or Murfreesboro properties with strong ARV upside. We present numbers with compassion, coordinate court-approval language with your attorney, and handle the logistics heirs should not have to — clean-out, personal property, and final walk-throughs across Davidson, Rutherford, and Wilson counties.

No showings while you are grieving. No renovation arguments. Just transparent comparison and a coordinator who answers when you text. Pair this with our House Clean-Out service and your Guardian Kit for a complete picture.

How we help families in Middle Tennessee

  • Nashville-area probate heirs overwhelmed by repairs — as-is exit without listing showings
  • Wilson County lake homes — multiple vetted buyers, never a single wholesaler lowball
  • Sumner County families needing certainty before school-year job transfers out of state
