You should not have to become a probate expert while you are still grieving.

Court-aware probate property guidance for Middle Tennessee heirs.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

Losing someone you love is hard enough. Discovering you also inherited a house — with a mortgage, deferred maintenance, siblings who disagree, and a court process you have never navigated — can feel like a second wave of overwhelm. That is exactly why Probate Guardians TN exists. We are probate real estate specialists, not generalist agents chasing listings. Every conversation starts with compassion, not a pitch.

We work alongside your probate attorney (never replacing them) to understand authority to sell, timeline constraints, and what the property actually needs before anyone talks numbers. You receive a complimentary CMA and Net Sheet written for heirs — real proceeds after debt, taxes, repairs, and carrying costs — so Nashville-area families in Davidson and Williamson counties, and heirs across Wilson and Sumner, can compare options on one page.

Your free Guardian Kit lays out four paths: as-is cash, funded repairs at closing, full MLS listing, or retain-and-rent — always subject to court approval. We coordinate vendors, insurance, clean-out, and competing buyers so you are never the project manager of your own grief. When you are ready — today or six months from now — we are one call away.

How we help families in Middle Tennessee

  • Helping Sumner County families understand Gallatin-area probate property timelines without pressure
  • Davidson County heirs in Hermitage and Antioch — vacant-home insurance and lockbox coordination
  • Wilson County siblings who need neutral Net Sheets everyone can trust at the kitchen table

  • Same compassionate tone as your Family Roadmap — zero obligation to decide today

Local examples

A Wilson County family in Mt. Juliet called us three days after funeral services — three siblings, one inherited rancher, and no idea who had authority to change the locks. Branton met them at the kitchen table that week; Scott coordinated a Net Sheet and attorney introduction before anyone talked about listing.

In Hendersonville, Sumner County, an out-of-state heir was losing sleep over a vacant childhood home. We secured insurance, ordered lawn care, and delivered a Guardian Kit comparing cash vs. funded-repair paths — all while their Davidson County attorney handled court filings.
