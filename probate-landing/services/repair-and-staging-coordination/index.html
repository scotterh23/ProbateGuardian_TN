Repair & Staging Coordination

Funded repairs at closing — list at peak value without heir arguments.

Call or text (615) 669-7075

Many Middle Tennessee inherited homes need roof work, HVAC attention, paint, or flooring to reach full market value — but grieving families rarely have spare cash or emotional energy to manage contractors. Funded repairs at closing solve that: approved work gets done, investment is repaid from sale proceeds, and siblings stop debating who pays for the Brentwood bathroom tile.

Probate Guardians TN solicits bids, schedules work, and compares the repair-then-list path against as-is cash on your Net Sheet — so Franklin and Murfreesboro heirs see net proceeds, not just a higher list price. Light staging and professional photography are added when ARV upside in Williamson or Davidson justifies the spend.

Court approval and contractor liens are handled with your attorney in the loop. Explore this path beside Seller's Agent Services and our county market guides — then call when you want real numbers, not guesswork.

How we help families in Middle Tennessee

  • Williamson County heirs maximizing Franklin and Brentwood ARV without upfront renovation loans
  • Davidson fixer-uppers in Antioch — roof and HVAC funded, repaid only when the house sells
  • Wilson County siblings who disagree on repairs — one coordinator, three contractor bids, one decision sheet
  • Sumner lake homes needing cosmetic refresh before spring listing season
  • Staging and photography when Inherited Home Sales MLS path wins on math
  • Always compared to cash offers so no one feels pushed into renovations

Local examples

A Franklin, Williamson County colonial needed HVAC and paint to reach Brentwood-competitive pricing — but heirs had no cash and less energy for contractor drama. Funded repairs at closing added $47K to list price; siblings repaid the investment only from proceeds.
