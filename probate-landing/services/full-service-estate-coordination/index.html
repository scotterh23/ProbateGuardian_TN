Full-Service Estate Coordination

Stop being the accidental project manager of grief — we coordinate everything else.

Probate scatters tasks like confetti: attorneys, insurance, utilities, lawn care, estate sales, sibling group texts, and a house that still needs to be secured. Most families have never done this before and should not have to learn on the fly while mourning. Full-Service Estate Coordination assigns you one Project Coordinator at Probate Guardians TN who owns the checklist — vendors dispatched, milestones tracked, attorney loop-ins sent.

Weekly status updates mean heirs in Portland, Clarksville, or Brentwood always know what happened this week and what is scheduled next — no chasing down contractors or wondering if anyone changed the locks. We present four property paths with real Net Sheets, coordinate clean-out and repairs, and keep the compassionate tone of your Family Roadmap from first call to closing.

This is the hub service many Middle Tennessee counties families start with — one number, one coordinator, one Guardian Kit. You focus on family; we handle the house burden.

  • Sumner families — estate sale, haul-off, and insurance vendors from one vetted rolodex
