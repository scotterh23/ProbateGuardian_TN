Probate Family Roadmap – 7 Simple Steps to Make This Season Easier

You don't have to navigate probate and an inherited home alone. This roadmap is plain English, zero pressure, and built for real families in Middle Tennessee.

Take it one step at a time. There is no rush. When you're ready for hands-on help, one call to Probate Guardians TN and we handle the property side — paperwork, repairs, cash offers, funded repairs, or a full listing — with real care.

Seven steps

  1. 1
    🌿

    Pause and breathe — you don't have to solve everything today

    Grief and paperwork at the same time is overwhelming. Give yourself permission to move slowly. Start by gathering basics: the death certificate, any will, and the property address. One folder, one step at a time.

  2. 2
    🏠

    Secure the home and protect the estate

    Lock up, forward mail, keep basic insurance on a vacant home, and agree on one calm point of contact among heirs. Small steps now prevent costly surprises later.

  3. 3
    ⚖️

    Meet with a probate attorney — we coordinate, never give legal advice

    Your attorney handles the court. We work alongside them on the property side only — valuations, buyers, listings, and timelines — always subject to court approval where required.

  4. 4
    📊

    Get real numbers — free CMA and Net Sheet for every heir

    Not a Zillow guess — a clear picture of what the estate would actually net after debts, costs, and repairs. Complimentary, no obligation, so the whole family works from the same facts.

  5. 5
    📋

    Ask about Muniment of Title if the estate may qualify

    A Tennessee shortcut many families don't know about — when there's a valid will, no unpaid debts (except those secured by real estate), and heirs agree, it can save months. Your attorney decides; we help coordinate property timelines.

  6. 6
    🛤️

    Choose your path — list, cash offer, funded repairs, or hold

    Compare options with zero pressure: traditional listing for maximum value (with funded repairs), vetted cash buyers through our investor network, or take more time. Your timeline, your choice.

  7. 7
    🤝

    Let us handle the heavy lifting — you focus on family

    Your personalized Guardian Kit, vendor network, showings, buyers, attorney loop-ins, and closing — we carry the load so you can breathe. One call gets everything moving with compassion and clarity.
