What we take off the desk

Property work only. Your office stays on authority, filings, and the legal file.

Vacant-house risk

Locks, lawn, utilities, and vacancy-policy condition while the estate is open — so the file is not also a property-management problem.

Local eyes + photo set

An in-person walk and a dated photo set the same week, written for the executor and easy to drop in the file.

List vs cash net sheets

Listing net and cash net on one page. Counsel is copied. The executor decides; we do not practice law.

Clean-out coordination

Keepsakes set aside or shipped, then donations and haul-away, with vendors met at the property. No charge to the firm.

Weekly written status

A short email the staff can file — what happened this week, what is scheduled next — without another phone tree.

Two paths. Counsel copied.

The executor sees listing and as-is cash on the same document. Your office gets a copy.

Marketed listing

Probate-aware timing, optional funded repairs, and a written net. Details for families are on inherited home sales.

As-is cash

Independent cash offers shown next to the listing net — same day, same page. See cash offers for probate properties.

Where we stand: we are licensed real estate agents, not the buyer. Cash offers come from independent buyers on eXp Realty's Express Offers network. eXp vets who gets on that list. You'll see those offers next to what a listing would likely have returned — same document, same day — and your attorney is welcome to review all of it before you decide anything.

How a referral works

Four steps. Your client stays out of state if they want to.

  1. 1

    Send the executor name and property address

    A call, text, or the short form below is enough. We will not contact the client until you say to.

  2. 2

    We assess in five business days

    Eyes on the house, a dated photo set, and both nets in writing — copied to counsel.

  3. 3

    The executor decides. Counsel gets a copy.

    List or as-is cash. We do not push a path. Your office stays on authority and court dates.

  4. 4

    Close around court dates

    We stay on the property work through closing and can work remote signing. Sales remain subject to court approval where required.
